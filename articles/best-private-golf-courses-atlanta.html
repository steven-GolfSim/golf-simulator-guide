Best Private Golf Courses in Atlanta

Atlanta's private golf scene runs through some of the most historically significant courses in the Southeast. The clubs on this list range from a Donald Ross design with deep ties to Bobby Jones and the TOUR Championship to a two-campus member club that spans Midtown and suburban Roswell.

Published November 26, 2026 · Approximately 3 minutes

Quick answer: Four private courses stand out in the Atlanta area: East Lake Golf Club, Atlanta Athletic Club in Johns Creek, Ansley Golf Club with locations in Midtown Atlanta and Roswell, and Druid Hills Golf Club. All four require membership or authorized guest access to play.

East Lake Golf Club

East Lake is a historic Donald Ross design located in Atlanta with deep ties to Bobby Jones and a long association with the TOUR Championship. In 2024, the course underwent a restoration led by Andrew Green that refined several holes while preserving the layout's original character. Access requires club authorization, and attending the TOUR Championship does not grant playing privileges.

Atlanta Athletic Club

Atlanta Athletic Club sits in Johns Creek and offers a substantial practice complex, a short par-three course, and an instruction center alongside its main courses. The range of on-site facilities gives members more variety in a single visit than most private clubs in the region can match. Confirm membership or hosted guest authorization before visiting, and check which specific course your access covers.

Ansley Golf Club

Ansley operates across two locations: a Midtown Atlanta site and the Settindown campus in Roswell. That two-campus structure is unusual for private clubs in the area and gives members access to distinctly different settings and course conditions. Note that an invitation to the Midtown location does not necessarily extend to Settindown, so confirm access for the specific campus you plan to visit.

Druid Hills Golf Club

Druid Hills Golf Club dates to 1912 and sits in one of Atlanta's most established neighborhoods. The club carries over a century of institutional history and maintains a private setting that reflects the character of the surrounding area. Access is limited to members and authorized guests, and visitors should review the club's published guest guide before arriving.

What to know before visiting

All four courses are private. Access requires either a membership or an invitation from a current member. Before visiting, confirm the dress code, cart policy, guest fee structure, and any tee-time restrictions. Do not assume that reciprocal club arrangements or attending a tournament at one of these venues will grant access for a round.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play these courses without being a member?

Only as an authorized guest of a current member. Some clubs may have reciprocal arrangements with other private clubs, but that varies and should be confirmed directly.

Which course has the strongest championship history?

East Lake Golf Club has the deepest tournament history, including its long-running association with the TOUR Championship and its connection to Bobby Jones.

Are there good public courses in Atlanta if I cannot get private access?

Yes. Atlanta has well-regarded public and municipal courses, including Chastain Park. For those options, see the companion guide to public courses in Atlanta.
