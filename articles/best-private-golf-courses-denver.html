Best Private Golf Courses in Denver

Denver's private golf courses sit at the edge of the Front Range, where the terrain, elevation, and water features create layouts that play differently from what you find in most other parts of the country. The clubs on this list each bring something distinct, from compact creek-lined courses in the city to restored championship designs in the surrounding suburbs.

Published November 25, 2026 · Approximately 3 minutes

Quick answer: Four private courses stand out in the Denver area: Denver Country Club, Cherry Hills Country Club in Cherry Hills Village, Lakewood Country Club in Lakewood, and Columbine Country Club in Columbine Valley. All four require membership or a member invitation to play.

Denver Country Club

Denver Country Club sits within the city itself on a relatively compact piece of land. A local creek runs through the property and comes into play on roughly a third of the holes, which gives the course a natural water-hazard element that most urban layouts lack. The tight footprint means shot placement matters more than raw distance, and the creek crossings add genuine risk-reward decisions throughout the round.

Cherry Hills Country Club

Cherry Hills is a William Flynn design from 1922, located in Cherry Hills Village just south of Denver. The club has a deep national championship history that spans several decades of professional and amateur events. The course is often highlighted for its distinctive par-5 green complex and demanding finishing holes. Access requires a private invitation, and guest arrangements need to be confirmed directly with the club.

Lakewood Country Club

Lakewood's architectural lineage is unusually layered. The course traces its design roots from Tom Bendelow through Donald Ross, and in 2017 it underwent a restoration led by Gil Hanse. That combination of original Ross character and modern restoration work makes it one of the more architecturally interesting private courses in the region. The club sits in Lakewood, just west of Denver.

Columbine Country Club

Columbine is located in Columbine Valley, south of Denver. The course features water-lined approaches, tiered greens, and several holes that present clear risk-reward choices. The green contours in particular are noted for being more demanding than what most Denver-area courses offer. It is a solid option for players who value short-game variety and approach-shot precision.

What to know before visiting

All four courses are private. Access requires either a membership or an invitation from a current member. Before visiting, confirm the dress code, cart policy, guest fee structure, and any tee-time restrictions. Do not assume that reciprocal club arrangements or living in the area will grant access to the course for a round.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play these courses without being a member?

Only as a guest of a current member. Some clubs may have reciprocal arrangements with other private clubs, but that varies and should be confirmed directly.

Which course has the strongest championship history?

Cherry Hills Country Club has hosted the most significant national events, including multiple major championships over the decades.

Are there good public courses in Denver if I cannot get private access?

Yes. The Denver area has well-regarded public and municipal courses. For those options, see the companion guide to public courses in Denver.
