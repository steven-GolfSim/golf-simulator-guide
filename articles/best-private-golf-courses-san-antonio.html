Best Private Golf Courses in San Antonio

San Antonio's private golf scene covers a wide range, from long-established clubs in the city's historic neighborhoods to newer developments that take advantage of the Texas Hill Country terrain just outside town. The five clubs on this list each stand out for a different reason, whether that is design pedigree, the sheer scale of the property, or a setting that feels closer to a retreat than a standard suburban club.

Published November 27, 2026 · Approximately 3 minutes

Quick answer: Five private courses stand out in the San Antonio area: Oak Hills Country Club, The Dominion Country Club, The Club at Sonterra, San Antonio Country Club, and The Clubs of Cordillera Ranch near Boerne. All five require membership or an approved guest arrangement to play.

Oak Hills Country Club

Oak Hills Country Club has been part of the San Antonio golf scene since the late 1920s. The course has hosted PGA Tour events over the years, including the Texas Open, which gives it a competitive pedigree that most private clubs in the area do not carry. Located near the Alamo Heights neighborhood, it sits in one of the more established parts of the city. The layout rewards shot-making over distance, and the mature tree-lined corridors give it a traditional feel. Access requires membership or an approved guest arrangement.

The Dominion Country Club

The Dominion is a gated community club on the northwest side of San Antonio. The course was designed by Bill Johnston and sits within a residential development, but the routing uses elevation changes and natural rock outcroppings in a way that gives it more character than a typical neighborhood layout. The Dominion has hosted Champions Tour events, which puts it in a small group of San Antonio courses with professional tournament history. Confirm membership and guest policies directly with the club.

The Club at Sonterra

The Club at Sonterra operates 36 holes across two courses in the Stone Oak area of north San Antonio. Having two courses at a single club gives members more variety and flexibility than most private facilities in the region. The setting includes rolling terrain and Hill Country views without requiring the longer drive to clubs further outside the city. It is a good fit for members who want volume and options close to the north-side suburbs.

San Antonio Country Club

San Antonio Country Club is located along the San Antonio River in the Olmos Park area and has been operating since 1907. That history makes it one of the oldest clubs in the city. The course has undergone redesign work over the decades, but it retains the kind of established, well-maintained character that comes with more than a century of continuous operation. Its central location makes it one of the more accessible private clubs in terms of proximity to downtown.

The Clubs of Cordillera Ranch

Cordillera Ranch sits outside San Antonio in the Boerne area, about 30 minutes northwest of the city. The Jack Nicklaus Signature course is the headline draw, with dramatic Hill Country elevation changes and long views that set it apart from any course inside the city limits. The broader club includes a resort-scale amenity package beyond golf. It is the furthest from downtown San Antonio on this list, but the setting and design make the drive worth considering for members who prioritize the landscape and course quality over convenience.

What to know before visiting

All five courses are private. Access requires either a membership or an invitation from a current member. Before visiting, confirm the dress code, cart policy, guest fee structure, and any tee-time restrictions. Do not assume that reciprocal club arrangements or living in the San Antonio area will grant access for a round. Private means permission, not a public tee time.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play these courses without being a member?

Only as an approved guest of a current member. Some clubs may have reciprocal arrangements with other private clubs, but that varies and should be confirmed directly.

Which course is the best value?

Membership costs and structures differ significantly across these five clubs. Contact each one for current initiation fees and monthly dues, as published pricing is rarely available for private clubs.

Are there good public courses in San Antonio if I cannot get private access?

Yes. San Antonio has well-regarded municipal and public courses, including several operated by the city. For those options, see the companion guide to public courses in San Antonio.
