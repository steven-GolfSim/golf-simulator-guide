How to Build a Golf Simulator at Home

A good home simulator begins with the room, not the shopping list. Measure carefully, set a realistic budget, and choose equipment that works together.

1. Measure your space

Check the room's width, depth, and ceiling height. Leave comfortable clearance for your full swing and enough distance between the hitting position, screen, and projector.

2. Choose the essential equipment

  • A launch monitor suited to your available space
  • A durable hitting mat
  • An impact screen or practice net
  • A projector and computer or mobile device
  • Compatible simulator software

If you're new to simulator technology, it helps to understand how golf simulators work before choosing your launch monitor, screen, and software.

3. Plan around your budget

Prioritize accurate shot tracking and a safe hitting area. A projector and enclosure can be upgraded later, while a reliable launch monitor and quality mat have the greatest effect on everyday use.

4. Test before final installation

Temporarily position every component and hit shots with each club you intend to use. Confirm swing clearance, screen safety, sensor readings, and projector alignment before mounting anything permanently.
