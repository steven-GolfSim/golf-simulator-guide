Golf Simulator Pricing: Hourly Rates by State

A data-driven benchmark of what indoor golf simulator bays actually charge per hour across the US, drawn from venue pricing pages tracked continuously by GolfSim.co. This is a summary of the full, continuously updated report.

Data snapshot: September 2026 · Approximately 3 minutes

Quick answer: The national median hourly simulator bay rate is $40. The middle half of venues charge between $25 and $55 per hour. A smaller premium tier pulls the national average up to $51, noticeably above the median.

The national number

The median hourly bay rate across tracked venues is $40. Because a smaller number of premium venues charge significantly more, the national average sits higher at $51. When comparing prices, the median is the more representative figure for what a typical booking costs; the average is skewed upward by the high end of the market.

The typical price range

The 25th to 75th percentile range runs from $25 to $55 per hour. That range reflects everything from budget walk-in bays with simpler setups to premium venues with high-end launch monitors, lounge seating, and full bar service. A venue quoting well outside this range, in either direction, is worth a closer look at what is actually included.

What the full benchmark covers

  • The national median and average hourly rate, with the 25th to 75th percentile range
  • Median and average hourly rates broken down by US state
  • Median and average hourly rates broken down by metro area
  • The full national hourly-rate distribution

States and metros with too few priced venues to be statistically meaningful are omitted from the benchmark rather than estimated, so every figure reported reflects real, published pricing.

Why prices vary so much

Pricing tends to track local cost of living and commercial real estate costs more closely than population size alone. A venue in a high-rent metro area faces different overhead than one in a smaller market, and that shows up directly in the hourly rate. Technology tier, amenities, and whether the venue leans toward entertainment or serious practice also factor in, though location remains the strongest single driver of price differences.

Methodology

Rates are auto-extracted from each venue's own website and treated as approximate. They refresh as venues are re-checked, so the benchmark reflects current, real-world pricing rather than a static snapshot. No artificial minimum or maximum is applied anywhere in the dataset.
