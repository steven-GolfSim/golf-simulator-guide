Best Private Golf Courses in Los Angeles

Los Angeles is home to some of the most storied private golf courses in the country. From venues that have hosted major championships and will stage the Olympics to quieter historic clubs tucked into the city, the LA area offers private golf with real depth and character.

Published November 20, 2026 · Approximately 3 minutes

Quick answer: Four private courses stand out in the Los Angeles area: Los Angeles Country Club (North), Riviera Country Club, Wilshire Country Club, and Annandale Golf Club in Pasadena. All four require a membership or a member invitation to play.

Los Angeles Country Club: North

LACC's North course gained wide attention when it hosted the 2023 U.S. Open, won by Wyndham Clark by a single stroke over Rory McIlroy. The layout features hole strategies that were restored to test shot-making at the highest level. It sits in the heart of LA, just off Wilshire Boulevard, which makes the contrast between the city outside and the golf inside especially striking.

Access requires going through authorized club channels. Tournament attendance or proximity to the club does not grant playing privileges.

Riviera Country Club

Riviera is one of the most recognized golf courses in the world, and its calendar reflects that. It has been selected to host golf for the 2028 Olympics and is the confirmed site of the 2031 U.S. Open. The course sits in Pacific Palisades and has long been home to the Genesis Invitational on the PGA Tour.

Playing Riviera requires club authorization. Watching a tournament there or visiting the neighborhood does not provide a tee time.

Wilshire Country Club

Wilshire is an urban course with a history that traces back to the Rancho La Brea land grant. A natural wash runs through the property, giving the layout a shape that feels less manufactured than many city courses. It is a quieter option compared to LACC or Riviera, but it carries real historical weight and sits in a part of the city where private green space is rare.

Only invited guests of current members may play. Access must be arranged through a member host.

Annandale Golf Club

Annandale has been open since 1906 and sits in Pasadena, slightly removed from the main LA golf corridor. Two-time PGA champion Paul Runyan once served as the club's golf professional, which gives the place a direct connection to competitive golf history. The club is often described as a family-oriented setting with a strong tradition of member involvement.

Membership is invitation-only. Authorized guests are expected to follow the club's published dress, arrival, and caddie procedures.

What to know before visiting

All four courses are private and require either a membership or an invitation from a current member. Before visiting, confirm the dress code, whether carts are available or if the club is walking-only, the guest fee structure, and any tee-time restrictions. Do not assume that living nearby or attending a tournament grants access to the course for a round.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play these courses without being a member?

Only as a guest of a current member. Some clubs may have occasional reciprocal arrangements with other private clubs, but that varies. Contact the club directly to ask about guest access.

Which course has the strongest tournament pedigree?

Riviera and LACC both have deep major championship histories. Riviera's upcoming Olympic and U.S. Open hosting puts it at the top of that list for the next several years.

Are there good public courses in LA if I cannot get private access?

Yes. Los Angeles has well-regarded public and municipal courses as well. For those options, see the companion guide to public courses in Los Angeles.
